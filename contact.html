Fees

I charge reasonable fees but have not adhered to the "convention médico-mutualiste". Fees vary according to the services provided.

The initial evaluation lasts 90 minutes and costs €220.

Time spent in evaluation, treatment, collaboration and report writing may be billed. Some activities may be captured in codes that can lead to reimbursement by various insurances.

New patient requests

Email info@drpaunicapsychiatry.be with the patient's name, date of birth, phone number, email address and a preferred 90-minute window from the Doctoranytime agenda. Dr. Paunica will enter and confirm the first appointment.

For a child or adolescent, a parent must obtain a referral letter from the family physician before the evaluation.

How to request an appointment

Not for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112 now or go to the nearest emergency department. In Belgium, free, anonymous support is available 24/7 from Zelfmoordlijn 1813 in Dutch and the Centre de Prévention du Suicide on 0800 32 123 in French. You can call simply because things feel bad and you want someone to talk to.
