Services

Not a menu. A plan built around you.

Care begins with understanding, then builds a plan that may include psychotherapy-informed work, medication, lifestyle stabilization and coordination.

What I offer

Five kinds of work — one continuous plan.

01

Comprehensive evaluation

I clarify symptoms, risk, history, medical contributors, medication history, development, family context, school or work pressures and cultural factors. I use diagnostic assessment when useful and give clear next-step recommendations.

02

Treatment planning

I organize care around you rather than around a label. Treatment may involve psychotherapy-informed care, lifestyle stabilization, sleep and routine work, family or school context — and medication when clinically necessary.

03

Medication, when needed

I consider medication as one part of a broader plan, not the whole plan. Decisions depend on clinical need, history, risk, tolerability and your context.

04

Collaboration

When useful — and with your consent — I may coordinate with psychologists, general practitioners, schools, employers' medical services or other care providers.

05

Spiritual exploration

I make room for spirituality as part of meaning-making. It can emerge from the alchemy of our relationship — with particular attention to presence, and the quality of that presence.

A patient-friendly summary

Four domains of care — held together by the quality of presence.

BiologicalSleep, medication, nutrition, exercise, energy, addiction issues, medical conditions and body rhythms can affect how you feel and function.
PsychologicalPsychoeducation, psychotherapy-informed care, family context, coping patterns, attachment, grief and meaning may be part of the formulation.
Social & culturalSchool, work, expatriate stress, language, culture, community and other care systems can shape both symptoms and recovery.
SpiritualValues, meaning and soul-searching — and how exploring the spiritual dimension can mobilize motivation for change.

How to request an appointment

Not for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112 now or go to the nearest emergency department. In Belgium, free, anonymous support is available 24/7 from Zelfmoordlijn 1813 in Dutch and the Centre de Prévention du Suicide on 0800 32 123 in French. You can call simply because things feel bad and you want someone to talk to.
