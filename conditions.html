Read in plain language

What brings people here — and where to read first.

01

Depression

More than low mood. Energy, concentration, sleep, motivation, appetite, relationships and a person's sense of future can all be affected.

→ 02

Anxiety

Understanding the alarm loop. Worry, panic, avoidance, perfectionism, irritability, body tension, sleep disruption and difficulty making decisions.

→ 03

Trauma & PTSD

Safety before speed. Stabilization, pacing, emotional safety, body awareness and careful integration into daily life.

→ 04

ADHD & attention

Attention, focus, organization and restlessness — in children, adolescents and adults. Careful assessment comes before any label or medication.

→ 05

Other mood disorders

Mood swings, bipolar-spectrum concerns and mood changes linked to health, medication or life transitions.

→ 06

Addictions

Alcohol, substances, screens and other patterns that take over — approached without judgment, with the body and the context in mind.

→ 07

Childhood & adolescent concerns

Development, school, emotions, behaviour and family life — with parents involved at the right distance.
