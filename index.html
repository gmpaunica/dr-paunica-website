Trained across three systems of care — practicing in one place.

I am board certified in Adult and in Child & Adolescent Psychiatry in the United States, trained in France, recognized in Belgium, and certified in integrative psychiatry. I work with the international communities around Brussels in English, French and Romanian.

For many years I trained in evidence-based, allopathic medicine. In recent years my work has deepened toward self-development, spiritual development, energy and vibrational medicine — above all, toward the quality of presence I bring to each encounter, so clinical knowledge can stay in the background while the desire for change comes forward.

The approach

A crisis can begin — a process of individuation.

My approach begins by creating an alliance in which you can feel at ease. Treatment starts when you enter the room: our relationship becomes a catalyst for self-knowledge and introspection. The medical assessment is one perspective; after our first meeting, I formulate your situation across four connected levels of analysis, each informing a corresponding level of intervention.

BiologicalSleep, energy, medication, medical history and the body's rhythms.
PsychologicalEmotions, beliefs, development, attachment and inner life.
PsychosocialFamily, school, work, culture, relationships and support.
Values & spiritualityMotivation, meaning, soul-searching and the spiritual dimension.
